Xbox Stick Calibrator

Permanent thumbstick calibration for Xbox Series X|S controllers, from Chrome on Android. Free. Same commands as the Xbox Accessories app.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome on Android (or Chrome/Edge on a computer).
Before you start
  1. Controller firmware 5.19.3221.0 or later (update it in the Xbox Accessories app or on the console).
  2. Plug the controller into the phone with a USB-C cable (or OTG adapter).
  3. Tap Connect, pick the controller, then Check (read-only) before Calibrate.

Calibrating erases each stick's stored calibration as soon as it starts. Finish every step. If you stop part-way, the controller uses its defaults until you calibrate it again.
